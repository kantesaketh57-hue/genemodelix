About the Journal

Overview

Genemodelix Journal of Integrative Biosciences is an international peer-reviewed publication under Ayugenixz Biosolutions Press Group, serving as a communication channel for research at the intersection of molecular biology, artificial intelligence in medicine, computational modeling, and biotechnology.

Access Model

The journal follows a Hybrid Open Access model, publishing under ISSN 2026-8941 (Print) and 2026-902X (Online).

Author & Submission Guidelines

Word limits for accepted submission formats:

Article Type Maximum Word Count
Original Research Article 7,000
Review Article 10,000
Short Communication 3,000
Mini Review / Case Study 4,000
Methods / Software Tool Article 5,000
Database Article 5,000
Editorial / Letter to Editor 1,500 - 2,000

File Format: Microsoft Word (.doc or .docx)
Font: Times New Roman, Size 12, Double spacing, A4 page, 2.5 cm margins.
Figures: Separate TIFF, PNG, or JPEG files (min 300 dpi).
Reference Style: APA 7th Edition.
Structure: Title Page, Abstract, 4–8 Keywords, Main Text, References.

Review Process: Double-blind peer review by at least two independent expert reviewers.
Review Timeline: Accept invitation within 5 days; submit detailed review within 21 days.
Evaluation Criteria: Originality, Scientific Significance, Methodology, Statistical Integrity, Results, Ethical Compliance, and AI Disclosures.

Note: No submission fee is charged. Article Processing Charges (APCs) are payable only after peer review and formal acceptance.

• Original Research Article: ₹5,000
• Review Article: ₹6,000
• Short Communication: ₹3,000

Plagiarism Threshold: Acceptable similarity index is below 15% overall.
AI Policy: AI tools may be used for language improvement but must be explicitly disclosed. AI tools cannot be listed as authors.
Copyright: Authors retain copyright under Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY 4.0) license.
